About Our School

Benevolent Islamic Secondary School in Makeni combines strong academics with Islamic values, giving every learner a balanced foundation for life.

🕌

Our Mission

Provide quality education rooted in Islamic faith, discipline and service to the community.
🌟

Our Vision

A school that produces morally upright, skilled and confident citizens for Sierra Leone and beyond.
🤝

Our Values

Honesty, respect, benevolence, hard work, and love of learning.

Academics & Student Life

A full curriculum supported by caring, dedicated teachers.

📚

Core Subjects

English, Mathematics, Integrated Science, Social Studies, ICT.
📖

Islamic Studies

Qur’an, Hadith, Fiqh, Seerah and Arabic language.
🔬

Senior Streams

Clubs & Sports

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for admission?

Go to the Register page, fill in the form and submit. The school will contact you.

Which classes are available?

JSS 1–3 and SSS 1–3, with Science, Commercial and Arts streams in the senior school.

Is Islamic Studies compulsory?

Yes. Qur’an, Hadith, Fiqh and Arabic are part of our school programme.

How can I speak to the school?

Use the message form on the Contact page, or email the school office.
